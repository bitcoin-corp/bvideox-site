Claim your channel

We never mint for you. Prove the channel is yours, link it to your bWalletX, and then choose what to mint. Your videos stay on YouTube either way.

  1. Sign in with Google

    Use the Google account that owns the YouTube channel.

  2. Let us check the channel is yours

    Grant read-only YouTube access. We ask YouTube which channels you own and compare the channel id. We can't upload, edit or delete anything, and we drop the access straight after the check.

    Coming soon

  3. Connect bWalletX

    Binds the channel to your bWalletX identity key, so only you can mint and get paid.

  4. Mint your videos Coming

    Mint each video's record (title, date, link, description, fingerprint) for pennies, in your own name. Adding the full video file comes later, from your own upload or Google Takeout.

Claiming opens once Google sign-in is set up for bVideoX. You can still watch the channel page now.
