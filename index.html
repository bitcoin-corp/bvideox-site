Bring your channel to Bitcoin.

Claim your YouTube channel in the bVideoX studio, then mint a proof-of-ownership record for each video: one small, encrypted record in your own wallet, 1p per video plus the network fee. Your videos stay on YouTube.

  1. 1

    Connect bWalletX

    Your studio opens under your own name, like $yourname.

  2. 2

    Claim your channel

    Paste a short code into your YouTube channel description. No Google sign-in. We read your public channel and list your videos.

  3. 3

    Mint proof of ownership

    Pick one video, a few or all of them. Each gets a record with its title and original upload date, 1p per video, in one approval.
