What minting does
  • Each record keeps the title, description, date and link, locked with your wallet’s keys. Only you can open it. You can make any one public later.
  • 1p per video plus a small network fee, shown before you mint. Your wallet asks before anything is paid.
  • Your videos stay on YouTube. We never download or copy them.

Show your studio on a domain you own, like videos.yourname.com.

How to point your domain

At the company where you bought the domain, add one DNS record:

Subdomain (videos.yourname.com):
CNAME  videos  →  cname.vercel-dns.com

Whole domain (yourname.com):
A  @  →  76.76.21.21

DNS can take up to a day to update. Then we connect it to your studio. That last step is coming soon, so your domain won’t show your studio yet.
